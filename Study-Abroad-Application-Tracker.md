# ✈️ Study Abroad Application Tracker

A practical tracker to help students organize and manage their overseas university application journey from initial research to departure.

## 🎯 Step 1: Define Your Goals

* [ ] Choose your preferred study destination
* [ ] Select a course or program
* [ ] Set an estimated budget
* [ ] Identify your preferred intake
* [ ] Research career opportunities

## 🏫 Step 2: Shortlist Universities

For each university, record:

* University name
* Country and location
* Course name
* Entry requirements
* Tuition fees
* Application deadline
* Scholarship opportunities
* Official application link

## 📄 Step 3: Prepare Your Documents

* [ ] Academic transcripts
* [ ] Valid passport
* [ ] Resume / CV
* [ ] Statement of Purpose (SOP)
* [ ] Letters of Recommendation (LOR)
* [ ] English-language test score, if required
* [ ] Portfolio, if required
* [ ] Financial documents, if required

## 💰 Step 5: Scholarship Planning

* [ ] Research university scholarships
* [ ] Check eligibility criteria
* [ ] Note scholarship deadlines
* [ ] Prepare required documents
* [ ] Submit applications
* [ ] Track scholarship decisions

## 🛂 Step 6: After Admission

Once you receive an offer:

* [ ] Review the offer letter
* [ ] Complete university formalities
* [ ] Arrange finances
* [ ] Prepare student visa documents
* [ ] Submit the visa application
* [ ] Arrange accommodation
* [ ] Plan travel

## ✈️ Step 7: Before Departure

* [ ] Confirm accommodation
* [ ] Book your flight
* [ ] Organize important documents
* [ ] Arrange required finances
* [ ] Prepare essential items
* [ ] Save emergency contacts
* [ ] Keep digital copies of important documents

## 💡 Important Reminder

The **Study Abroad Application Process** varies depending on the country, university, and course. Always verify admission and visa requirements through the relevant official university and government websites.

This tracker is an educational resource designed to help students stay organized throughout their overseas university application journey.

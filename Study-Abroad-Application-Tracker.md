A simple tracker for students managing university applications during their study abroad journey.

 Choose preferred study destination
 Select course or program
 Set an estimated budget

Country and location
Course name
Entry requirements
Tuition fees
Application deadline
Scholarship opportunities
Official application link

📄 Step 3: Prepare Documents
 Academic transcripts
 Passport
 Resume / CV
 Statement of Purpose (SOP)
 Letters of Recommendation (LOR)
 English-language test score
 Portfolio, if required
 Financial documents, if required

The Study Abroad Application Process varies depending on the country, university, and course. Always verify admission and visa requirements through the relevant official university and government websites.

This tracker is an educational resource designed to help students stay organized during their overseas university application journey.
